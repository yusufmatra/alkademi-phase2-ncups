# Singapore Visa and Entry Guide for Indonesian Travelers

**Entry Requirements:**
Indonesian travelers should check the latest Singapore immigration requirements before departure. A valid passport, confirmed accommodation, return or onward ticket, and sufficient funds may be requested by immigration officers.

**SG Arrival Card:**
Eligible travelers must submit the Singapore Arrival Card electronically within the required period before arrival. Use the official Singapore immigration channel and keep the submission confirmation available.

**Visa Check:**
Visa requirements depend on nationality, travel document, and purpose of travel. Confirm the current requirement through Singapore's official immigration information before booking or flying.

**Length of Stay:**
The immigration officer determines the permitted stay at entry. Travelers must leave Singapore before the permitted stay expires unless they receive an approved extension.

**Travel Advice:**
Singapore applies strict rules to prohibited goods and public conduct. Check customs requirements, carry prescribed medication with supporting documents, and keep passport and travel documents secure.
