# South Korea Travel Guide for Indonesian Travelers

**Entry Documents:**
Indonesian passport holders should check the latest entry requirements before departure. Prepare a valid passport, accommodation details, return or onward flight information, and any required electronic travel authorization or visa documents for the planned trip.

**Payment:**
International Visa and Mastercard cards are widely useful in Seoul and other major cities. Carry a small amount of Korean won for smaller shops, markets, and transport top-ups. Inform your bank that you will use the card in South Korea.

**Transportation:**
The Seoul subway and bus network are practical for visitors. A rechargeable transit card can be used for public transportation. Keep the destination address in Korean when using taxis or asking for directions.

**Connectivity:**
Travelers can use an eSIM, SIM card, or portable Wi-Fi. Keep the passport and booking information available when collecting a rental SIM or device.

**Travel Advice:**
Bring comfortable walking shoes, check the weather before each day, and confirm opening hours for attractions. Keep emergency contacts and travel insurance details accessible.
