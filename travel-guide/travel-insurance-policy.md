# Travel Insurance Policy Guide

**Recommended Coverage:**
Travel insurance should cover emergency medical treatment, emergency evacuation, trip cancellation or interruption, travel delays, baggage loss, and baggage delay according to the policy terms.

**Before Purchasing:**
Check the coverage limit, deductible, destination area, policy period, exclusions, and pre-existing medical condition rules. The policy period should cover the complete journey, including the departure and return dates.

**Making a Claim:**
Contact the insurer as soon as possible after an incident. Keep medical reports, receipts, police reports for theft, airline documents for delays, booking confirmations, and proof of payment.

**Important Exclusions:**
Coverage may be limited or excluded for undeclared pre-existing conditions, unsafe or prohibited activities, alcohol-related incidents, travel against medical advice, and claims without required supporting documents.

**Emergency Assistance:**
Save the insurer's emergency assistance number before departure. In a medical emergency, seek local emergency help first and then notify the insurer as soon as practical.
